### 4.3 The dark palette as an accessibility decision
> The near-black ground was a brand choice, but it tests well: white on `#040606` measures 20.31:1, far above the 4.5:1 AA threshold. Argue it deliberately — a dark interface also suits a night event and reduces glare in low light. Full table in the README.
